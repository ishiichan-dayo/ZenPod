ZenPod is a music manager for iPods that works with both the stock iPod OS and Rockbox. Drag and drop songs onto your iPod. No iTunes needed.

ZenPod in 54 seconds

What it does

Put music on your iPod and tidy up the files on your PC, in the same window.

The Rockbox database, built on your PC

Every time you add songs, ZenPod builds a database in the same format as Rockbox 4.0 on your PC. Eject the iPod and browse by artist and album under “Database” right away. Play counts and ratings are kept.

Edit song info

Fix titles, artists, track numbers and sort names, on the iPod or in the original files. Look albums up on MusicBrainz and fill everything in at once.

Tidy up your music files, too

Add your music folders and work on them in the same window as your iPod: edit tags, get song info from MusicBrainz, find artwork and rename files in bulk. You don’t even need an iPod for this.

Videos and photos

In iPod OS mode they go into Movies, TV Shows and Photos; in Rockbox mode into the Videos and Pictures folders. Videos the iPod can’t play are converted.

Browse and tidy your iPod

Switch between songs, albums and artists; play, edit, rate, build and reorder playlists, and copy songs back to your PC.

Just drop to transfer

Drop songs or album folders onto the window. Drop onto a playlist to add them there too.

Any format

In iPod OS mode, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and others are converted automatically (lossless to ALAC, lossy to AAC 256 kbps). Rockbox gets them as is.

Artwork included

Uses embedded images, or cover.jpg and similar files in the same folder.

Podcasts and audiobooks

They remember where you stopped. Subscribe to a feed and send the episodes you haven’t heard yet.

Smart playlists

Combine rules just like in iTunes. Import from iTunes XML and .m3u8.

Automatic updates

The app tells you when a new version is out and updates in one click.

iPod OS and Rockbox, side by side

The same action works differently in each mode. On a dual-boot iPod with Rockbox, you can keep songs for both on one iPod.

FAQ

Do I need ffmpeg?

Only to convert FLAC and other formats in iPod OS mode, and to convert videos. MP3, AAC, ALAC, WAV and AIFF don’t need it, and songs in Rockbox mode usually don’t either.

On Windows and macOS, install it in one click from the welcome guide or from Settings, under Conversion (ffmpeg). FLAC on macOS converts even without it. On Linux, install it from your distribution’s packages.

Can I still use iTunes?

Yes, and songs added with iTunes show up in ZenPod. But if iTunes or Music.app syncs automatically, it may remove songs added with ZenPod. Set it to “Manually manage music”.

Can I go back if something goes wrong?
  • iPod OS: the first time an iPod is opened, its original database is saved as iTunesDB.zenpod-backup. Copy it back to iTunesDB to restore.
  • Rockbox: before the first rebuild, the original database is saved in .rockbox/zenpod-db-backup/. You can also rebuild it on the iPod with “Database → Update Now”.
  • Databases are written to a temporary file first and then swapped in, so an interrupted write is unlikely to corrupt them.
Does the app send anything?

To improve the app, once a day it sends anonymous usage data (app version, OS, the iPod’s mode and model, a rough range of its song count and so on) to a server in Japan run by the developer. No song titles, file locations, iPod names or serial numbers are sent, and IP addresses aren’t stored. Turn it off any time in Settings, under About. The full list is in the README.

How do I add songs?
  1. Connect your iPod over USB and open ZenPod. It finds the iPod automatically.
  2. If Rockbox is installed, choose the mode with “iPod OS | Rockbox” at the top left.
  3. Drop songs or album folders onto the window.
  4. When you’re done, click “Eject” before unplugging.
Where do I report bugs?

In GitHub Issues. Your iPod model (shown at the top left of the window), the mode and your OS help a lot.
